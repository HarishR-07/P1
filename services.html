Home / Services

From first sketch to final handover.

Six ways we take a space from idea to occupied — design, manufacturing and execution, handled by one accountable team.

01 / Workspaces

Corporate & Commercial Interiors

We design offices that add to the creativity and productivity of the teams working in them — space planning, workstation layouts, meeting rooms and breakout areas built around how your business actually operates.

  • Space planning & layout design
  • Workstation, cabin & meeting room design
  • Branding integration in reception & common areas
  • MEP coordination & statutory compliance
02 / Residential

Home Interior Design

We customize homes to meet your styling and functional requirements, delivering interiors with speed, flexibility and transparency — from single rooms to full-home turnkey projects.

  • Concept design & 3D visualization
  • Modular kitchens, wardrobes & custom furniture
  • Material & finish selection support
  • Full execution and handover
03 / End-to-End

Turnkey Fit-Outs

A single point of contact oversees the entire project — minimizing the need to deal with multiple vendors and reducing coordination stress for your team.

  • One team across design, civil, MEP & furniture
  • Fixed-scope, transparent quoting
  • Single project manager as point of contact
  • Snag-free handover process
04 / Manufacturing

Furniture Manufacturing

Our own factory in Manesar builds customizable furniture in-house, so your project isn't waiting on a third-party vendor's production schedule.

  • Workstations, executive & reception tables
  • Ergonomic seating
  • Custom sizing & finish options
  • Faster lead times than outsourced sourcing
05 / Planning

Space Planning & Vastu

Layouts that balance efficient space usage with Vastu principles where requested — helping the space feel considered from every angle.

  • Efficient headcount-to-area planning
  • Vastu-aligned layout options
  • Circulation & way-finding design
  • Future-growth flexibility built in
06 / Visualization

Virtual 3D Walkthroughs

See your space before it's built. We produce realistic 3D previews so design decisions get made early — not after materials are already on site.

  • Photoreal 3D renders
  • Walkthrough videos for stakeholder sign-off
  • Material & lighting previews
  • Fewer change orders during execution
