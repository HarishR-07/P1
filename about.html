Interior architects who design for how people actually work.

Divine Innovation is a corporate and commercial interior design firm based in Manesar, Gurgaon — with our own furniture factory, an in-house design team, and projects delivered across Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Bangalore and Noida.

Our story

Built by architects, backed by our own factory.

Divine Innovation started with a simple observation: most interior design firms hand off the "build" part of a project to a chain of vendors they don't control, and the client absorbs every gap in that chain — in timelines, in quality, and in cost. We set out to do it differently, by keeping design, furniture manufacturing and on-site execution under one roof.

Today our design team works out of Manesar alongside our own furniture workshop, which means a chair, workstation or reception counter can go from drawing to delivery without ever leaving our supply chain.

How we work

Every project starts with understanding how a space is actually used day to day — not just how it should look in a render. From there our architects produce layouts, 3D walkthroughs and material boards before a single wall moves.

  • In-house design, space planning and 3D visualization
  • Own furniture manufacturing unit in Manesar — no third-party sourcing delays
  • Single point of contact from concept to handover
  • Post-handover support for snags, additions and refits
